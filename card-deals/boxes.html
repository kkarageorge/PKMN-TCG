How this works
  • Left out: Chaos Rising, Perfect Order, Pitch Black (your completed sets).
  • Set codes are the official expansion numbers; 30th Celebration has none, so it shows 30C (its Fetch and TCGplayer code).
  • Auctions: eBay AU auctions ending within 48 hours, current bid + postage to 6103, per pack. Never bid on by Claude.
  • Kollecter (Perth: Bayswater, Belmont, Carousel, Whitfords, Ellenbrook) is $0 postage with free click & collect. Its pre-orders are left out: only paid Klub members can buy them, and Klub sign-ups are paused.
  • Hidden as likely fakes (eBay only): anything under 55% of the set's usual price per pack, and eBay sellers with under 10 feedback or under 97% positive.
  • Pack counts come from the product type (booster box 36, Pokemon Center ETB 11, ETB 9 (8 for Sword & Shield), bundle 6, build & battle 4, 3-pack blister 3) or the pack count in the listing title. Collections and tins without a stated pack count are left out.
  • Per pack is for buying one listing. Loose packs from the same seller usually share one postage charge, so several work out cheaper.
  • Japanese, Chinese and other non-English products, cases and multi-box lots are left out.
